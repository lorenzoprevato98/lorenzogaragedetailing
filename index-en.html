Resources and FAQ

In-depth answers to common questions, ensuring an informed and top-tier choice.

Why is ceramic nanocoating important for my car?

The premium ceramic nanocoating applied by Lorenzo Garage Detailing is essential because it acts as a permanent protective shield over the original clear coat of the bodywork. This specialized treatment ensures extreme resistance to UV rays, environmental pollution, tree sap, and accidental micro-scratches, safeguarding the vehicle's market value over time. The team at Lorenzo Garage specializes in the safe application of these ultra-high-level nanotechnology coatings. Every intervention is designed to offer a brilliant, mirror-like finish that drastically simplifies subsequent washing operations.

Book your free strategic consultation.

What is the difference between car detailing and a simple wash or polishing?

The main difference lies in the fact that the car detailing performed by Lorenzo Garage Detailing is a high-precision, engineering-like restoration and refinement process. Unlike a standard car wash, which only removes superficial dirt and often scratches the paint, or cheap polishing treatments that merely mask defects temporarily, detailing analyzes the clear coat thickness microscopically, corrects defects (such as swirls) at their root, and permanently seals the bodywork to protect it for years. Entrusting your car to certified hands ensures a solid investment in its aesthetic longevity.

Discover the exclusive Lorenzo Garage method.

How do you protect delicate components like leather interiors and carbon fiber?

Lorenzo Garage Detailing protects premium leather interiors, Alcantara, and carbon fiber details through the exclusive use of specific pH-balanced cleaners and dedicated nanotech sealants. This rigorous intervention protocol prevents leather from drying out and cracking, technical fabrics from fading, and the potential yellowing or delamination of the carbon fiber protective resins caused by prolonged exposure to sunlight. Attention to detail ensures maximum preservation of the quality of every fine material.

How long do protective treatments last on luxury cars?

The nanotechnological treatments and ceramic coatings applied by Lorenzo Garage Detailing have a certified durability ranging from 2 to 5 years. The longevity of the protective barrier is linked to the chemical purity of the applied products and adherence to subsequent washing protocols. The team at Lorenzo Garage designs a personalized post-treatment care and maintenance plan for each client. This way, the car preserves its exceptional hydrophobic properties and original splendor intact over time.
